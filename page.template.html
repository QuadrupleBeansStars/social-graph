4 · So what

Business takeaways

Starbucks is talked about for price, not taste

In the network, Starbucks sits in the cluster with ราคา (price), แพง (expensive), แบรนด์ (brand), สาขา (branch) and ลูกค้า (customer). Its one big spike was the May 2026 launch of “Es Yen”, priced under 100 baht to compete with local chains.

→ Value pricing is what gets Starbucks noticed. Measure how many people trade down, not only how many people mention it.

Café Amazon is talked about for its drinks

Café Amazon sits with กาแฟ (coffee), แก้ว (cup), นม (milk), หวาน (sweet) and ชง (brew). Other threads are about its sales record (455 million cups, OR's quarterly results) and collaborations such as the Dek Somboon soy-caramel drink.

→ Collaborations get people talking. Sweetness is a recurring point to watch.

Convenience coffee is gaining attention

Mentions of 7-Eleven / All Café rose steadily from Sep to Nov. Mixue and other Chinese chains appear mainly in threads about the market being saturated and branches closing.

→ For a premium chain, the competition is the ฿40 cup at 7-Eleven. Track it every month.

Most posts are about home brewing and cost

One of the most discussed threads asked what the 53 baht goes to when a 60-baht latte only uses about 7 baht of coffee. Home espresso machines and roast level (light, medium or dark) form their own large cluster.

→ Pantip users are cost-conscious. Content that is open about pricing could work well here.
Method & limits
  • Source: the 50 most recent threads for each of 10 Pantip tags, plus all comments under them. Pantip tag pages only show the latest 50 threads, so recent months have more data than older ones. The rise in Oct–Nov overall volume is partly caused by this.
  • Brands: a post counts as mentioning a brand when it contains a Thai or English spelling of the name. Inthanin, Black Canyon and True Coffee came up too rarely to chart.
  • Network: Thai words were split with PyThaiNLP (newmm), filler words were removed, and the most frequent words were kept. Two words are linked when they appear in the same post at least 2 times in the period (4 times for "All months").
  • Sentiment: I tried a simple word-list method, and it labeled about 85% of posts neutral, so it isn't shown here. Real sentiment needs a Thai model, for example one trained on the Wisesight corpus.
