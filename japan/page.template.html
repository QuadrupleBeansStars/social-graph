What Thai travellers talk about on Pantip, season by season

Japan travel threads and their comments on Pantip from January 2025 to November 2026, compared with official monthly counts of Thai visitors from the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO).

1 · Pantip vs. actual trips

Thai visits peak three times a year. Pantip posts don't follow that pattern.

The bars show Thai visitors to Japan each month, according to JNTO. They peak in March–April (sakura season and the Songkran holiday), in October and in December (autumn leaves and New Year), and are lowest in August. The line shows Pantip posts and comments.

2 · Which places in which season

Hokkaido is talked about in winter, Tokyo in summer, and Osaka and Kyoto in autumn

Each post is assigned to the season of the trip if the title names a month, and to the season it was posted otherwise. Each cell compares how often a place or topic is mentioned in that season with how often it's mentioned overall. 1.0× is average, and darker cells mean the season is above average for it. Only months with full data are used (Jan 2025 – Sep 2026).

4 · So what

Business takeaways

People talk about a season months before it

Autumn-leaves talk is about as high in summer (1.6×) as in autumn (1.7×). Sakura talk peaks in winter (1.3×), before spring itself (1.1×). People ask about a season 2–3 months before it.

→ Start promoting autumn trips in June–July and sakura trips in December–January, not when the season begins.

Winter means Hokkaido and driving

Hokkaido (2.1×), Sapporo (2.3×), snow (2.6×), renting a car (2.0×) and the JR Pass (2.0×) all peak in winter. Many questions are about driving on snow and how to get around.

→ For a travel agency: sell Hokkaido packages that include a car with snow tyres, or a guided driving route.

Summer is the off-season for travel, but Tokyo and onsen are discussed most

JNTO shows August is the lowest month for Thai visitors. Even so, Tokyo (1.5×), Nagoya (2.1×) and onsen (1.6×) are mentioned most in summer.

→ Offer off-season Tokyo and onsen deals when flights and hotels are cheaper.

Cost questions come up in every season

"Budget/yen" (336 mentions) and "hotel" (239) are the two largest topics, and they stay near 1.0× in every season. Cost is a concern all year.

→ Content that compares real costs ("a 7-day Osaka trip for ฿35,000") fits how Pantip users plan.
Method & limits
  • Pantip: the 50 most recent threads from each of about 15 Japan tags (Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Hokkaido, Sapporo, Fukuoka, JR Pass, เที่ยวญี่ปุ่น and others), plus every comment under them. Threads only about anime, Japanese food in Thailand, or Thais living in Japan were left out.
  • Uneven coverage: the busiest tags (the main Japan tag, Tokyo) only reach back a few weeks or months, so Oct–Nov 2026 has far more data. Those months are shaded in chart 1 and left out of the season tables.
  • Places and topics: detected with lists of Thai and English spellings. Other words were split with PyThaiNLP (newmm), and filler words were removed.
  • JNTO: official monthly counts of visitors from Thailand (the 訪日外客数 spreadsheet). The 2026 figures are provisional.
  • This shows patterns in recent tagged threads. It is not a random sample of Pantip, so it can't measure market share.
